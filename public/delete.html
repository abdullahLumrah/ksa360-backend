Delete your KSA 360 account

This page is for the KSA 360 app by Lumrah. Use it to request that your account and associated data be deleted.

How to request deletion

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open KSA 360 and sign in
  2. Go to Profile
  3. Tap Manage profile
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm

On this page — if you signed up with email, enter the same email and password below. You do not need the app installed.

Google-only accounts have no password here. Delete them in the app, or email privacy@lumrah.co from that same Google address.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email, phone, date of birth, gender, and profile photo
  • Sign-in sessions and push tokens
  • Your marketplace ads, chats, job posts, community pages, posts, comments, and likes — after the retention window below

What we keep, and for how long

  • The account is deactivated immediately. You cannot use the app until you restore it.
  • You have 15 days to sign in again and restore the account.
  • After 15 days the account and the personal data above are permanently deleted.
  • We may keep limited records needed for safety, fraud, or law (for example a deletion log without your profile).
  • Public content other people still need to understand a thread may stay in anonymized form, or be removed with the account.
