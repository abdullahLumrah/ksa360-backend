KSA 360 · Lumrah

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

KSA 360 is an all-in-one app for expatriates in Saudi Arabia (guides, food, play, marketplace, jobs, communities, prayer times, and daily tools). It is operated by Lumrah (www.lumrah.co). This policy explains what we collect and why.

1. Who this covers

People who browse as a guest, create an account, sign in with Google or email, post in the marketplace or community, or use admin tools.

You must be 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

2. Data we collect

Account (you give this to us)

  • Name and email
  • Password (stored as a hash, never as plain text) if you use email sign-in
  • Saudi mobile number (+966), gender, and date of birth — entered in the app, not taken from Google
  • Profile photo if Google provides one

Google sign-in

  • We receive your Google name, email, and profile photo only
  • We do not request Google birthday or gender

What you post

  • Marketplace ads, photos, and in-app chat messages
  • Community pages, posts, comments, likes, and reports
  • Job posts and guide submissions

Location

  • City you pick, or GPS if you tap “Use my location”, to show prayer times and nearby food, play, clinics, and schools
  • We use this to personalize nearby results. We do not sell location data

Usage

  • App section opens, sign-in / sign-out, and similar events so we can see how the app is used
  • A device identifier if you are not signed in, so counts stay unique

3. How we use it

  • Create and keep your account
  • Show your profile, ads, chats, and communities
  • Moderate content and keep the marketplace / community safe
  • Improve the app and understand unique usage
  • Contact you about your account if needed

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Where it is stored

The app API and database run on Amazon Web Services. Your account and posts live in our database on that server. Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.

5. Who can see it

  • Other users can see what you choose to make public (name on posts and ads, ad photos, community content)
  • Lumrah admins can see accounts and content to operate and moderate the service
  • Google sees the sign-in you start with them
  • If you use Ask KSA, your question is sent to our AI helper to generate an answer

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account while it is active. If you delete your account in the app, it is deactivated immediately and permanently removed after 15 days unless you sign in again to restore it.

7. Your choices

  • Edit name, mobile, birthday, and gender in Manage profile
  • Delete your account in Manage profile
  • Decline location permission; you can still pick a city by hand
  • Email privacy@lumrah.co to ask what we hold or request deletion

8. Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, and signed login tokens (JWT) for your session. No method is perfect. Protect your password and your phone.

9. Saudi and other law

If you use the app in Saudi Arabia, the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) may apply. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data using the contacts below.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the new policy.
